# Changelog

What changed in OpenJK-rpmod from version to version, newest first. What each feature does now is in [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md), its commands in [COMMANDS.md](COMMANDS.md).

OpenJK-rpmod is built on [OpenJK](https://github.com/JACoders/OpenJK); for the changes OpenJK itself made, see [its changelog](https://github.com/JACoders/OpenJK/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md).

## 0.9.0 - Nov 9th 2026

- [Added] `HoloNet and Comport on the datapad`. The **HoloNet** reads JEDI's news from jediholo.net: the latest articles, each opened whole with its pictures. The **Comport** shows JEDI's forum as datapad pages: forums, topics, posts with quotes and pictures; log in with your Comport account to read everything and reply to topics. Your password goes only to the forum, over HTTPS, and is never kept; Stay logged in keeps only its cookies.
- [Added] `Pazaak on the datapad`. KOTOR's card game against another player on your server, through C1B3R: start a table or join one, play a match to three sets, or watch one. Build and save your 10-card side decks (kept on C1B3R). C1B3R deals and checks every move, so no one sees your hand. The cards are drawn by the client, with Jedi Academy's sounds; your own art and sounds in a pk3 are used instead if you have them. `/datapad pazaak` opens it.
- [Added] `Datapad`. `/datapad` opens a datapad like a phone: it rises in the bottom right corner, from above the HUD down to the screen's edge, with the mouse, and Esc or `/datapad` again puts it away; an app that needs the room has it bigger on the right side. `/datapad pos <x> <y>` moves it. Its top bar shows JEDI's own time (`474.08 ABY - 1345/49 GST`, New York's time) and names show RPMod's colours. Its home screen has apps, starting with **Settings**: its colour, the keys that open and close it, and whether your character holds a datapad (RPMod's `/emlift`) while it's open.
- [Added] `C1B3R: everyone with the client connected`. The tools' hooks (cinematics, map edits, NPC walks and talk, objectives, the sky, shared shaders and effects...) go through C1B3R only, never through `rpnotify` any more: every player with the client is connected and receives them the moment they're sent. To send, an admin links their game once: `/c1b3r link` while logged in to their RPMod account, then `/link <code>` in the project's Discord, and staff approve it there. The game keeps a token, and sends while logged in to the account it was linked with; everyone else receives. NPC talk comes through it too, in the chat box as before. `/c1b3r` shows your link, `/clientusers` who's connected. Clients from before, still on `rpnotify`, no longer reach this one, nor it them. `cl_hookGap` is gone.
- [Added] `Hooks only for players with the client`. The hidden messages the tools share what they do with (NPC walks and talk, cinematics, objectives, map edits, the sky, emotes...) no longer go to everyone, where players without the client saw them as `cmd:` lines: they go only to the players with this client, one after the other, as the server's flood protection lets them through. Who has it is known from the project's feedback bot, which each game tells which server and client number it's on, kept in memory only. If the bot doesn't answer, a hook isn't sent. `/clientusers` lists who has the client.
- [Added] `Chat log`. `/chatlog`, also in the in-game menu's TOOLS, keeps the chat in `chatlogs/` as you play: a `.txt` and an `.html` for each day on each server. The page looks like the console, with every colour, RPMod's too, and shows or hides each kind of chat. It can start by itself on joining a server, and tells, team chat, whispers and comms, OOC, emotes and admin chat each go in or not. **Open page** and **Open folder** open them.
- [Added] `Cinematic manager: extras`. **+ Extra** on the Entities tab adds someone only this client draws: any model and skin, with a saber, a staff or two sabers in any colour, no NPC spawned on the server. Extras walk, run, play animations and emotes, act out recordings, say lines, change their look, appear and go, and the camera can follow them, like any player or NPC in a cinematic; screens with this client see them when it's played for everyone.
- [Added] `Cinematic manager: camera stops, faces, map edits at a moment, slow motion, several bars at once`. A path's spot can be one the camera **Stops here** at, easing in and out. People talk as they say a line, and a Look gives them a face (smiling, frowning, alert, eyes closed). A map look can show a Map Editor layout meanwhile, its props hidden or moved and its textures changed. The World tab's **Time** page plays stretches in slow motion or faster. Ctrl+click picks several bars on the timeline to drag together.
- [Added] `Cinematic manager: dolly zoom, a second more or less, loop, picture`. A shot keeping someone in frame can **Dolly zoom**, the vertigo shot. **+1 s** and **-1 s** on the timeline put time in or take it out at the moment, everything after moving along. **Loop** previews between the marks either side of the moment, round and round. **Picture** saves the moment as the players see it to `screenshots/`.
- [Added] `Cinematic manager: the map's look, game sounds`. The World tab's **Map** page darkens, brightens or colours the whole map's light, colours the sky and lays fog over everything for a while, faded in and out, one look blending into the next (night falling, an alarm, a haze). **Game sounds** turns the game's own sounds down or off while a cinematic plays, its music as it is.
- [Added] `Cinematic manager: force powers, a camera flown by hand, marks, bar sizes`. A **Force** action plays lightning, drain, grip, push, pull, heal or rage from someone's hands at someone else, with both of them acting it. **Record a shot: fly the camera** keeps a camera move as you fly it. **+ Mark** marks moments on the timeline that the moment and dragged bars snap to. Bars can be thin, normal or wide.
- [Added] `Effect Replacer`. `/effectreplacer`, or the effect manager's new Mine tab, plays the effects of your own force powers, saber and weapons as any other effect you have: your Drain as a blue fire, while everyone else's Drain stays as it is. Use a power, and the effects that just came from you are listed to pick from; the replacement plays live before you swap it. For every player, not only admins, and kept for every map. Shared *(experimental)*, everyone with this client who has the replacement sees yours on you.
- [Added] `Esc Menu: Modern and KOTOR`. A menu with room for everything, in two looks, opened with `/escmenu` or, once picked, with Esc (the game's own Classic bar stays the default): **Modern** (categories down the side, cards you scroll, search as you type) or **KOTOR** (a framed screen with tabs along the top, like KOTOR's). Every tool, your character's pages, JEDI's servers, the camera settings, and the game's own Controls, Setup and Renderer pages opened straight there. **Menu Style** (or Setup > Game Options > Esc Menu, `cl_escMenu`) picks Modern, KOTOR or the game's own Classic bar; `/escmenu` opens it anytime.
- [Added] `Cinematic manager: music, screen effects, only the cast, held props`. **+ Music** on the Sounds tab plays a track from the music manager in place of the map's for a while or to the end. The World tab's **Screen** page adds flashes, tints faded in and out (or a fade to black) and dark edges. **Seen: only those in it** hides everyone else while it plays. A **Look** can put any model in someone's right or left hand, turned to sit right.
- [Added] `Cinematic manager: undo, copy and paste, video`. Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo every change, a drag at a time. Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V copy and paste a shot, an action, an effect, a sound, a text, a prop or a light at the moment picked; an action pastes onto whoever is picked, and with no action picked all that person does is copied. **Video** (or `/cinematicmanager video <name>`) records the cinematic to an .avi in `videos/`, every frame at `cl_aviFrameRate`, with nothing of the manager over it.
- [Added] `Cinematic manager: props and lights`. A new World tab puts models in the scene for a while, going through keys you place at moments of the cinematic, smoothly or straight, turned and sized as you like (a ship setting down, a crate falling), and lights at a spot or on someone, any colour and reach, steady, flickering, pulsing, strobing or a flash dying away, faded in and out. Each has its own row on the timeline; seen on screens with this client.
- [Added] `Cinematic manager: Look`. A new action changes how someone looks for as long as its bar lasts: hidden, their size from 25% to 400% with their saber still in hand, drawn as a hologram or cloaked or with a shell (force glow, light or dark side, electric, red or yellow glow, force protect), and their saber off, another colour, or its blades longer or shorter. Seen on screens with this client.
- [Added] `Cinematic manager: keep someone in frame, and shake`. A shot's new Track page names a player or NPC to keep in frame: a path turns to them wherever they go, an orbit circles them as they move, and turned with them it stays behind, beside or in front of them, a chase camera, kept out of the walls. **Handheld** sways the camera as if held by hand, and **+ Jolt** shakes it at a moment, strength 1 to 10, dying away.
- [Added] `Cinematic manager: Text`. A new Text tab and timeline row put words on the screen at any moment of a cinematic, not only before or after it: a line said on the bottom bar with who says it, a title on the top bar, a center message or a notification in the top left, in any colours, three sizes, and typed out a letter at a time if you like. They show as you write them at the moment picked on the timeline, and for everyone with this client when it's played for everyone. Asked for in /feedback #18.
- [Added] `Map editor: swap a prop`. In the Models tab, a prop of the map's, clicked or already lifted out, can be swapped for any model: pick it in the list and **Swap for ...** hides the prop and puts the model where it stood, its bottom on the prop's and as big as fits in the space the prop took, then turned, scaled and moved like any model. It's lit by the light around it, not the light baked into the map. Saved in layouts and shared with the other edits. Asked for in /feedback #16.
- [Added] `Special moves`. `/specialmoves`, also in the in-game menu's TOOLS, lists every special move of the saber: the katas, lunges and spins, death from above, the flips and leaps, back attacks, cartwheels and butterflies, kicks, rolls and wall moves, and the ones only a `.sab` gives. Each says what it does, how it's done by hand, the styles it's for, the force points and Force Jump it takes, and whether you can do it now. **Bind a key** gives a move a key that does it for you: its keys pressed for a moment, as you'd press them, so the server sees an ordinary move. `/specialmove <name>` plays one.
- [Changed] `Experimental features on Temple: Department Head, Council and God only`. On JEDI Temple, **TOGGLE EXPERIMENTAL** and `/rpexperimental on` work only for those admin ranks, asked quietly with `rpstatus` a few seconds after you join. Until the rank is in they stay off; for anyone else they're turned off, kept on with `/rpexperimental` too. `/toolaccess` shows whether your rank may have them there.
- [Fixed] `Custom emotes: shared ones stuck, or on two players`. A custom emote was put on any body near its player, so someone standing close played it too, and it was written into the player's own model: when it ended the bones could stay as the emote left them (with an animation the game didn't restart, as BOTH_STAND). It's now drawn on a copy of the player's own body, made each frame, in its place: their own model is never touched, so when it ends they're drawn again as the game has them. Each emote takes its own player's body only.
- [Fixed] `Cinematic manager: a video crashing at the end`. Recording a cinematic as a video crashed the game when it ended: the file was closed while the renderer still had its last frame to fill. It's now closed once that frame is done, and `/stopvideo` waits the same way.
- [Fixed] `Cinematic manager: the actions to add under the bottom buttons`. With an extra picked, its settings pushed the list from + Add action down under Preview and Play for everyone. The list now opens as a popup over the panel, above the button when there's no room below; a click elsewhere closes it.
- [Fixed] `Special moves: Force Jump 4 read as 0`. The server sends the Force Jump level in a way that only holds 0 to 3, so level 4 came as 0 and moves that need Force Jump said you didn't have it. Force Jump known at 0 is now taken as the 4 it is.
- [Fixed] `Cinematic manager: extras' animations`. An Animation or Emote action on an extra played on whoever stood nearest instead.
- [Removed] `Presets: All models`. The tab showing every model installed (and `/skinselect models`) is gone: each model shown stays loaded until the next map, so big model folders ran the 32-bit game out of memory. It may come back if RPMod goes 64-bit. `/skinselect` and `/skinselect <model>` still show a model's skins.

## 0.8.2 - Nov 7th 2026

- [Added] `Asset manager`. `/assetmanager` lists what's on the map (rpmodels, rpeffects, looping rpsounds, overlays and remapped shaders) and the pk3 each comes from, custom or the base game's, and packs the custom ones into a new pk3 in `assetpacks/` with what they need: shaders, textures, LODs, skins, skeletons, an effect's models and sounds.
- [Added] `Skin select`. `/skinselect` shows every skin of your model as a card, worn in a click; a model made of head, torso and legs parts gets a card to put them together a part at a time. **All models** shows every model installed, worn as it is or opened for its skins. Asked for in /feedback #15.
- [Added] `Presets: favorites, search and more pages`. A star makes a preset a favorite, **Favorites** shows only those, a search box finds them by name, model or saber, and there's room for 256, with first and last page buttons.
- [Added] `Storyteller: edit in the diagram`. Blocks are dragged to move them (an If with all of it, several picked together), Ctrl/Shift+click picks several, and copy, cut, paste, select all and delete work there. Its **Panel** shows the actions, dragged into the diagram where a marker shows the step would go, and the picked step's settings.
- [Added] `Storyteller: Center message placement and typewriter`. A centre message can start at the bottom of the screen or a number of lines down (the \\n's rpcentersay takes), and can be typed out a letter at a time, each with a sound picked in the sound manager.
- [Added] `Storyteller: team triggers`. A "when" or an If can count only one team's players, red or blue, as the server has them: only that team sets it off, so only one team moves the story on. Words and use triggers count by the team the player was on when they said or used them. Asked for in /feedback #13.
- [Added] `Storyteller: Go back and Mark`. Go back plays the story again from an earlier step: the "when" before it, which waits again, the start, or a Mark by its name. Under an Else, a wrong answer goes back to wait, so a console can be used again and again and only works for the right person. Asked for in /feedback #12.
- [Added] `Map editor: Textures`. A new tab gives the map's surfaces another texture where you pick, not everywhere as a shader remap does: a snowy Duneeden Temple with snow on the ground outside and the same stone inside left alone. Pointing at the map outlines what a click would pick and names its texture; a click picks the surface, the ones of its texture joined to it, or all of its texture (Shift+click adds or takes off). A texture pointed at in the list shows on the picked surfaces straight away, before it's kept; the new texture keeps the surface's lightmap, so it's lit as it was. Shaders that grow grass, bushes or flowers are marked **grows**, and grow them on what they're given to, with Vulkan too. **Grows on it** grows any sprite image (tall grass, flowers...) on any ground, the surfaces' own included, with its width, height and spacing; the renderer makes the shader for it, so clients without this see the ground as it was. Saved in layouts and shared with the other edits; clients without it see the map as it is. Needs the release's renderers, which are updated with it. Asked for in /feedback #9.
- [Added] `Custom emotes: keys`. A custom emote can now move: **Add key** under the bones poses them again at a later time (up to 10 keys), and the bones go smoothly from each key's pose to the next, the turns the short way round. Each key is picked to pose it, its time typed; the preview's < and > step through them and its bar marks them. **Copy this pose to it** goes into the picked key, so frames of a mod's animation copied a key at a time play it back for those without its .gla. Keys are saved and shared with the rest; a client from before them shows the first key's pose. Sharing also no longer loses a space where a long emote is cut into parts. Asked for in /feedback #6.
- [Added] `Custom emotes: Copy this pose`. On the Animations tab, the frame shown of any skeleton's animation (a mod's .gla on the humanoid's bones, a cutscene's, or the humanoid's own) is copied into the custom emote as bones turned and moved, so those with this client but without that .gla see the pose too. A skeleton with no model of its own now shows its animations copied onto your model. The animation manager also lists every skeleton found, not the first 32. Asked for in /feedback #10.
- [Added] `Renderer page: AO Strength`. Under Ambient Occlusion, Low, Medium or Strong: how dark it makes corners and creases (`r_ssaoIntensity` 0.3, 0.6 or 1). Medium is the default now; Strong is how it was.
- [Added] `Background audio`. **Background Audio** in Setup > Sound, or `/s_backgroundAudio 1`, keeps the game's sound playing with it minimized or another window in front, instead of turning it off until you come back (off by default).
- [Changed] `In-game menu: TOOLS in FEEDBACK's place`. Its panel lists every tool this client adds, Feedback with them, five a page with Back and Next, each with what it does and its command, and opens the one clicked.
- [Changed] `Admin flags line off by default`. The noclip, nodraw, nonsolid and notarget line at the bottom shows only with `cl_adminFlags 1` now.
- [Changed] `Vulkan: tone mapping that doesn't darken`. Tone mapping (`r_tonemap 1`) used a film curve (ACES) that also pushed the shadows down, so with it on the game looked darker all over on dim maps. `r_tonemap 1` (**Soft** on the RENDERER page) now only rolls off the brightest light and leaves the rest as it is; the film curve is still there as `r_tonemap 2` (**Film**), and in a map's `.grade` as `tonemap 2`.
- [Changed] `Renderer page: the effects under Post Effects`. With Vulkan, bloom, HDR, dithering, ambient occlusion and the second page's effects only work with Post Effects (`r_fbo`) on, so they show only then, under it; with it off they're out of the way, instead of seeming to do nothing.
- [Fixed] `In-game menu: menus over the camera panel`. With Camera Settings on, the menu Exit opens and the Tools and Quick Connect panels were hidden under the camera panel; the panel now steps aside while any of them is open, and Exit puts the Tools and Quick Connect panels away.
- [Fixed] `Admin flags line while spectating`. Following a player in noclip showed noclip on at the bottom as your own, and it stayed on after following someone else. The line now waits while you follow someone, and your noclip is read from the server both on and off, so it can't stick. Reported in /feedback #7.
- [Fixed] `Console text the same size with supersampling`. With Vulkan's supersampling on, the console and the lines over the game came out half their size, the picture being drawn at twice the window's size. They're now sized to the window, as is a render resolution of its own (`r_renderScale`). Reported in /feedback #11.
- [Fixed] `Custom emotes: the rotation rings no longer fold into each other`. With a bone turned (most of all pitched towards 90) the preview's Pitch, Yaw and Roll rings leaned into each other until they overlapped. They now stay square to each other, each one turns the bone round its own axis, and the three angles are worked out from that. Reported in /feedback #5.
- [Fixed] `Vulkan: HD video cinematics`. An mp4, webm, mkv or mov cinematic of a size other than a power of 2 (1920x1080...) stopped the game with Vulkan: "Draw_StretchRaw: size not a power of 2". They play as with OpenGL now.
- [Fixed] `Cinematics out of sync after alt-tab`. A cinematic's sound (`/rpcinematic`, a RoQ or an HD video) was paused with the window out of focus, and fell behind its picture, beeping, once you came back. It keeps playing now while a cinematic plays, whatever the setting.
- [Fixed] `Vulkan: "vkWaitForFences returned VK_TIMEOUT"`. With a second game started next to it, two clients at once, the graphics card can be kept busy for a while, and after 10 seconds the game closed with this error. It waits now, saying so in the console, and gives up only after a minute. A window restored from the taskbar at the wrong moment could also leave the card waiting for good, with the same error; not any more.
- [Fixed] `"Ran out of transform space for Ghoul2 Models"`. Near a very detailed character model, a saber hit or anything else that checks its exact shape could drop you to the menu with this error. The game has eight times the room for it now (2 MB, JKA's was 256 KB).

## 0.8.1 - Nov 3rd 2026

- [Added] `Feedback`. **FEEDBACK** in the in-game menu, between Quick Connect and Exit, or `/feedback`, opens a window to send a bug report or an idea for the OpenJK-RPMod project, in game or from the main menu: a title, and what happened or what the idea is. It goes with your RPMod account, so it's sent from an RPMod server, logged in; the game's version, renderer, map and server go with it. It's answered on the project's Discord, and marked planned or fixed, or closed. The answers come back under **My reports**, where you can answer them too, and the console says when one of your reports has news.
- [Changed] `NPC names under the crosshair: shared when the admin says`. An admin's client no longer reads or shares the NPC names by itself, as NPCs spawn or players join, so names not made for roleplay stay out of the way: `/npcnames` reads them and shares them as they are now with everyone with this client, the admin included, and `/npcnames clear` takes them back. Until then nobody sees them, admins neither.
- [Changed] `Quick connect: Esc closes the panel first`. With the quick connect panel out, Esc puts it away and leaves the in-game menu open; a second Esc closes the menu.
- [Changed] `Animation manager: going back a skeleton`. On the Animations tab a right click on the skeleton's button goes back one, and the arrow beside it lists every skeleton to pick one, so the humanoid is a click away.
- [Changed] `Admin flags on screen: hidden with the HUD`. The noclip, nodraw, nonsolid and notarget line hides with `cg_draw2D 0` or `cg_drawStatus 0`, as the rest of the HUD does.
- [Fixed] `Animation manager: bones left turned`. A bone turned or moved in a custom emote stayed that way after it: in the preview of every other animation and emote, even once the emote was deleted, and on a player's model after the emote played on it. They're let go now.
- [Fixed] `Vulkan: the map's light style looks`. A look given to the whole map's light (style 0: the map editor's Dusk, Night and the rest) left the world as it was with Vulkan: its lightmaps, and with world caching on (`r_vbo 1`) its vertex lit rocks and mountains too; all of it follows the look now, as with OpenGL. The grass and foliage sprites on the ground follow it too now, in OpenGL as well as Vulkan.

## 0.8.0 - Nov 3rd 2026

- [Added] `Discord status`. Your Discord profile shows you playing ::JEDI::: the server, your character, the players, the time, the map's picture, and a Join JEDI button. Off until turned on in Setup > Game Options, or `cl_discord 1`; `cl_discordPrivate 1` leaves the server and your character's name out.
- [Added] `Quick connect`. **QUICK CONNECT** in the in-game menu, before Exit: JEDI's Temple, Grounds, Region and Galaxy with their addresses, maps and players as they are now; one click and you're on your way.
- [Added] `Admin flags on screen`. While noclip, nodraw, nonsolid or notarget is on for you, a small line at the bottom says which: on in green, off in red.
- [Added] `Presets`. **PRESETS** in the in-game menu, or `/presets`: characters saved (name, model, skin, sabers and their colours) and shown as cards with the name in its colours, RPMod's too, the character standing, its skin, sabers and the date; one click and you're that character.
- [Added] `Camera settings in the in-game menu`. **Setup > Game Options > Camera Settings** shows the third person camera's settings beside the in-game menu: distance, angle, height, side, tilt, aim follow, see-through, field of view, the HUD, and defaults; while it's on the camera keeps right behind you, so it never swings round. They can be saved by name and loaded again (**<** and **>** go through them), or with `/campreset`.
- [Added] `Animation manager: turn and move bones on the model`. In a custom emote's preview the picked bone has rings for pitch, yaw and roll and arrows for X, Y and Z: drag them to turn and move it. Bones can be moved now, not only turned (it takes this client's own renderers, any of them).
- [Added] `Animation manager`. `/animationmanager` has three tabs. **Animations** shows every animation of every skeleton loaded, base or custom, playing on your model: paused, stepped frame by frame, dragged through, sped up or slowed down; each says whether it's the base game's or which pk3 added or changed it. **Emote chains** makes one command of several emotes, each starting when the last one's animation is over (`/emgreet` doing `/emsalute` then `/emyes`). **Custom emotes** *(experimental)* makes an emote of an animation, with bones following other animations or turned; an admin's, with experimental on, show for everyone with this client, anyone else's on their own screen. Both are saved and become `/em...` commands.
- [Added] `NPC names under the crosshair`. With the crosshair on an NPC that has a targetname, its name shows where a player's does (`cl_npcNames`, on). Only RPMod's admin-only `npc list` says who's who, so an admin with this client and experimental on reads it quietly as soon as an NPC spawns near them, and every 30 seconds, and shares the names with everyone with this client, and again for each player who joins.
- [Added] `Storyteller: diagram`. **Diagram** shows the story as a flowchart: its Whens, its Ifs splitting into a yes and a no side and joining again at End if, the stories it plays. Click a step to pick it, a Play story to open the story it plays; the wheel or the arrows scroll, Esc goes back.
- [Added] `Storyteller: who set it off, and Play story`. An If can ask who set off the When above it ("If it was Luke who set it off"), by the whole name or part of it. **Play story** plays another saved story alongside, so a lever's "When someone uses" can start Story2.
- [Added] `Cinematic manager: teleport, NPC commands, spawn and remove`. Four more actions for who's in a cinematic: **Teleport** (somewhere else at once; NPCs for real), **NPC command** (freeze, follow, dialog, hologram, kill), **Spawn** (an NPC that isn't there yet appears at its moment, its moves and lines following once it's there) and **Remove**. The NPCs' are sent by whoever plays it for everyone. An NPC to spawn is added on the Entities tab by the targetname it'll have.
- [Added] `Storyteller: use triggers in order`. A "When someone uses a trigger" step can say what its use trigger does while the story hasn't got to it yet (*Too early*): hidden from the players, or showing them a message ("Breakfast seems more interesting") instead of being used. So dinner can't be eaten before breakfast: the story's own order decides, and the trigger opens as soon as the story reaches its step. For players with this client, with the storyteller on the map.
- [Added] `Renderer settings in the Setup menu`. Setup has a RENDERER button under DEFAULTS, in the main menu and in game: the renderer (OpenGL or Vulkan), anti-aliasing and dynamic glow, and with Vulkan its post effects, bloom, HDR, dithering, world and model caching and ambient occlusion, and on a second page supersampling, soft particles, FXAA, sharpening, sun shafts and their strength, tone mapping and motion blur. Changes apply with the video page's Apply, as the rest of Setup's do. The button and page are added to whichever Setup menu the game has, RPMod's included, as it loads; nothing is shipped for them.
- [Added] `Vulkan graphics effects`. With Vulkan and `/r_fbo 1`, each off until turned on: ambient occlusion (`r_ssao`: corners, creases and the ground under things darkened), soft particles (`r_softParticles`: smoke, fire and other effects fade where they meet walls instead of cutting into them), FXAA (`r_fxaa`: smooths edges over the finished picture, grass and fences too), sharpening (`r_sharpen`), sun shafts (`r_sunShafts`: beams from the sun on maps whose sky has one), tone mapping and colour grading (`r_tonemap`, `r_exposure`, `r_saturation`, `r_contrast`, `r_warmth`, or a map's own `maps/<map>.grade`), and motion blur when the camera moves (`r_motionBlur`). The cinematic camera's depth of field blurs bright points into round discs (`r_dofBokeh`, on; 0 for OpenGL's look). Supersampling works (`r_ext_supersample 1`: drawn at twice the width and height, scaled down to the window; screenshots come out at that size), and soft particles work in fog. Setup's RENDERER page has a second page for these, *More Settings >*, with the sun shafts' strength and motion blur on it too.
- [Added] `Vulkan renderer` (experimental). ## 0.2.1 - Oct 11th 2026

- [Added] `Modelmanager`. This tool replaces modelplacer entirely (although the previous command has been kept as an alias momentarily until future updates). Model manager will allow you to edit existing one or multiple rpmodels in the server, delete them or group rpmodels together. You may save multiple models together in what is called 'a construct' that can be loaded at your convenience. An outline toggle (B hotkey) has been added to temporarily hide model outlines during placement/edit. You may as well choose to only apply the outline only for a single model (O hotkey). You may give models or a construct a name so that it may be removed with /modelmanager remove &lt;name>
- [Added] `State logic`. Similar yet not to be confused with rpdumps. States allow for additional information regarding a model or effect to be kept client-wise should the player quit the game whilst rpmodels/rpeffects are present in-game or should they be spawned later with a rpdump. Serves to allow removal of models or effects that have been named, as otherwise information of the name would be lost unless the player remains connected after set-up.
- [Added] `Npcmanager enhanced`. This tool now allows you to place multiple NPCs before submitting them to server, as well as save parametric configurations for NPCs and use them at a later time. Constructs are also applicable to NPCs now.
- [Added] `Effectmanager enhanced`. This tool now allows you to place multiple effects before submitting them to server. Constructs are also applicable to effects now.
- [Added] `Shadermanager custom input`. This tool now allows you to insert the new shader path yourself instead of selecting it from the list.
- [Added] `Manager browsers enhancement`. The manager UIs will now feature: specifics on where the assets come from, as well as the chance to filter only Base or Custom assets.
- [Added] `Free camera in npcmanager`. Allows the player to use a freecamera whilst placing NPCs around the map
- [Added] `Remap effect's shaders`. Allows the player to use shadermanager to remap effects, you may do so per each individual shader that is part of the effect.
- [Added] `Execs Unlocked`. You will no longer be stuck in waiting limbo while an exec is running, and you may run multiple of them. Additionally, you may use /execlist to list running exec's and run /execstop &lt;name> to stop them. If &lt;name> parameter is not provided, it will stop all running exec's.
- [Added] `Console Chat Range`. Allows the player to set a chat range for console much like the game's standard chat.
  - /rpc_chatRangeConsole_mode: 0 -> default, 1 -> fade by distance, 2 -> print outside console's maxchat range, 3 -> only below min range
  - /rpc_chatRangeConsole_min: messages closer than this many units is solid (128 default)
  - /rpc_chatRangeConsole_max: messages farther than this many units is at min. alpha or left out in mode 2 (1024 default)
  - /rpc_chatRangeConsole_alpha: How see-through chat console gets, in percent (25 default)
- [Added] `Crouch Toggle`. Allows the player to toggle themselves in a state of permanent crouching with /cl_crouch
- [Added] `Allow the client to accomodate up to 64 players`. This one was missing due to it being a fork of the standard OpenJK client. Now it should be up to par to what is being shipped with RPMod currently.
- [Fixed] `Selecting a skybox with shadermanager leads to a game crash.`.
